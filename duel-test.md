# Duel Test — Fix Human Sleep: Funniest Patch Note

## Method

Two rival drafters produced 28 candidate lines. A 6-line shortlist was
duel-tested round-robin, pairwise in both orders, under the kit judge brief
(real patch note fixing human sleep; funny on a single read; genuine
changelog voice; single line under the cap; specific surprise). Every
shortlisted line was also tested against 3 stock baselines.

Honest caveat: this judging was proxy — no OpenRouter key was available on
the build VM, so the duels were run under the kit judge brief without a live
judge call, and the blind rounds hid the live top-5 texts. The numbers below
are the tournament's recorded results, not a live-board measurement.

All six shortlisted lines beat all 3 stock baselines, by a margin of 3 or
more. B12 won the round-robin outright at 5-0 with the highest mean game
(0.68); B1 was the clear runner-up at 4-1 (0.60).

The build brief supplied the tournament results and the winning line, but
not the exact texts or character counts of the five non-winning shortlist
lines, so they are not reproduced here — only the recorded records above.
The winning line's exact text and 151-character count are given below.

## Critic's verdict and revision

A rival critic then attacked the round-robin winner B12 and found two
weaknesses:

1. Tone risk: B12 contained the phrase "childhood trauma", which could read
   as a cheap or off-key punch in a one-line patch note.
2. A loose tail: the ending did not callback to the setup, so the joke's
   second half carried less of the first half's energy.

The revision (R1) removed "childhood trauma" entirely and tightened the
tail into a callback joke — "no questions asked" now answers the permission
theme set up at the start of the line, so the punch lands on the same idea
the line opened with.

## Mini-duel: R1 vs B12 vs B1

The revised line R1 was re-duel-tested against the original winner B12 and
the runner-up B1, pairwise in both orders under the same judge brief.

- **R1: 2-0, mean game 0.65** — won the mini-duel outright.

Fallback order after the mini-duel: **R1 > B1 > original B12**.

## Final winner — R1

> Fixed sleep's permission bug: it no longer asks for location, contacts, and your browser history; it just starts when you lie down, no questions asked.

- **151 characters, 151 bytes**, single line, no trailing newline.
- Inside the competition gate (1–160 characters, UTF-8, not only
  whitespace).
- Why it wins: the permission-bug framing is a genuine changelog voice;
  the escalation (location → contacts → browser history) is specific and
  relatable; the tail callbacks to the permission theme instead of
  introducing a new idea, so the whole joke lands in a single read.
